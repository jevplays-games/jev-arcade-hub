Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-30

This page explains what the launcher and the games collect. Each game is a separate app and has its own records.

The launcher

This site has no accounts, sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising. Its server checks whether each game is online, which sends a plain request to that game's address and stores nothing about you.

The games

Games record matches on their own servers: moves, results, timestamps and, for each opponent decision, the request that was prepared and the answer chosen. This is what the analytics and decision-evidence views show. A game's opponent model is only sent the information needed to choose from a fixed set of legal options. It is not sent hidden information such as secrets, an opponent's layout, or your history.

Discord sign-in

If you sign in with Discord for ranked play, the game receives your Discord identity (ID and display name) to attach results to you. Games do not read your messages or your servers. You can revoke access at any time under Authorized Apps in your Discord settings.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal data. Data is processed by the hosting and model providers needed to run the games. Match records are kept to maintain rankings and analytics.

Your choices

You can play without signing in where a game allows it. To ask about or remove data tied to your Discord account, contact the operator through the Discord application listed for that game.
